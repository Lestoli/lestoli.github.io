    How to Play

    Draft

    • A coin toss decides who drafts first. Picks go 1 – 2 – 1: first player takes one character, the second takes two, the first gets the last.
    • ☀️ Sun starts on row 1 (B1, D1), 🌙 Moon on row 5 (B5, D5). The player who drafted second takes the first turn.

    Your turn

    • Click one of your characters, then click a glowing square to jump one step up, down, left or right.
    • You can't jump onto a broken square, another character, or a tree.
    • Every square starts with 3 HP (the gems on its edge). Landing deals it 1 damage. At 0 it breaks and falls out of the sky — along with whoever stands on it.
    • A red square (☠) has 1 HP left: jumping there sends your own character over the edge.
    • After the jump, the character's passive triggers.

    Winning

    • You lose when all your characters have fallen, or when it's your turn and none of them can jump.
