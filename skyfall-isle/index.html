    How to Play

    Draft

    • A coin toss decides who drafts first. Picks go 1 – 2 – 1: first player takes one character, the second takes two, the first takes the last.
    • Sun starts on the bottom row (B1, D1), Moon on the top row (B5, D5). The player who drafted second takes the first turn.

    Your turn

    • Pick one of your characters and jump it one square up, down, left or right.
    • You can't jump onto a broken square, another character, or a tree.
    • Every square starts with 3 HP. Landing on a square deals it 1 damage. At 0 it breaks for good — and whoever is standing on it falls.
    • That means jumping onto a 1-HP square (marked ☠) sends your own character over the edge.
    • After the jump, the character's passive triggers.

    Winning

    • You lose when all your characters have fallen, or when it's your turn and none of them can jump.
